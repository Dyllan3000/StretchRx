Shoulder Stretches

Shoulder Rolls

  1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and arms relaxed at your sides
  2. Begin by rotating your shoulders forward in large circles
  3. Then reverse the movement and rotate them backward
  4. Maintain a straight back and breathe normally throughout the exercise

Exernal Rotation Shoulder Stretch

  1. Stand Straight
  2. Grab a pole or something straight with one of your arms
  3. Keep your arm bent at 90 degrees
  4. Twist your body towards the opposite side

Cross Arm Stretch

  1. Stand with your feet slightly narrower than shoulder-width apart
  2. Raise your right arm to just below shoulder height
  3. Place your left hand on your right elbow and gently guide your right arm across your body, using the left hand to support it
  4. Hold this stretch for up to 30 seconds
