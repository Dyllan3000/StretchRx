Wrist Stretches

Resisted Wrist Flexion

  1. Sit or stand with your forearm supported on a table or your thigh, palm facing up
  2. Hold a weight (such as a dumbbell or resistance band) in your hand with your wrist hanging off the edge
  3. Slowly curl your wrist upward, bringing the weight towards your forearm
  4. Pause briefly at the top of the movement, then slowly lower the weight back down
  5. Repeat for the desired number of repetitions

Resisted Wrist Extension

  1. Sit or stand with your forearm supported on a table or your thigh, palm facing down
  2. Hold a weight (such as a dumbbell or resistance band) in your hand with your wrist hanging off the edge
  3. Slowly extend your wrist upward, lifting the weight away from your forearm
  4. Pause briefly at the top of the movement, then slowly lower the weight back down
  5. Repeat for the desired number of repetitions

Wrist Extensor Stretch

  1. Extend one arm in front of you with the palm facing down
  2. Use your other hand to gently press down on the fingers of the extended hand, stretching the wrist and forearm
  3. Hold the stretch for 20-30 seconds, then switch arms and repeat
