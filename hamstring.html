Hamstring Stretches

Knee-to-Chest Stretch

  1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and arms relaxed at your sides
  2. Bend one knee and bring it towards your chest, clasping your hands around your shin or behind your thigh
  3. Keep your other leg extended straight on the floor
  4. Gently pull your knee closer to your chest until you feel a comfortable stretch in your buttocks and lower back
  5. Hold the stretch for 20-30 seconds, then switch legs and repeat.

Standing Forward Bend

  1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart and your knees slightly bent.
  2. Slowly hinge forward at your hips, keeping your back straight as you reach your hands towards the floor or your shins
  3. Allow your head and neck to relax
  4. You can bend your knees more if needed to ease the stretch on your hamstrings
  5. Hold the stretch for 20-30 seconds while breathing deeply, then slowly return to the standing position

Wall Hamstring Stretch

  1. Begin by lying on your back with your buttocks close to a wall and your legs extended upward against the wall
  2. Keep your knees straight and your heels pressing against the wall
  3. Slowly slide your heels down the wall until you feel a gentle stretch in the back of your thighs (hamstrings)
  4. Hold this position for 20-30 seconds, then return to the starting position
  5. You can adjust the intensity of the stretch by moving closer or farther away from the wall
