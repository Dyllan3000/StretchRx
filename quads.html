Quad Stretches

Floor Quad Stretch

  1. Begin by kneeling on the floor with one leg positioned in front of you
  2. Bend the knee of the leg behind you and reach back to grab your foot with your hand
  3. Then gently pull your foot up towards your buttocks
  4. Hold this stretch for approximately 30 seconds before switching sides

Chair Quad Stretch

  1. Kneel on a chair and grasp one ankle
  2. Pull the heel towards your buttocks while simultaneously straightening your hip
  3. Hold this position for about 30 seconds before returning to the starting position.
  4. Switch to your opposite leg and repeat the same steps with your other leg

Standing Quad Stretch

  1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart, and find a sturdy surface like a table, countertop, or door frame for support
  2. Bend one knee, bringing it towards your buttocks, and grab onto your ankle
  3. Once you feel balanced, gently pull your foot towards your buttocks until you feel a comfortable stretch in the front of your thigh
  4. Repeat the same steps for the other leg
